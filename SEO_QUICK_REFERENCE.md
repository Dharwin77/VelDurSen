## 🔍 How to Verify

### 1. Check Meta Tags
View page source (Ctrl+U) and look for:
- `<title>VelDurSen - Enterprise AI Solutions...`
- `<meta name="keywords" content="Enterprise AI...`
- `<meta property="og:...` tags
- `<script type="application/ld+json">` (Schema.org)
